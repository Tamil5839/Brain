# Brain

**[A THOUGHT](a_thought/)**: a film of a fruit fly brain making a decision, rendered from
its complete published wiring (FlyWire, release 783). Every neuron sits at its true
position, and every flash of light is a spike from a whole-brain leaky integrate-and-fire
simulation that reproduces Shiu et al. (2024, *Nature*): sugar taste drives the feeding
motor neuron MN9, bitter does not, and bitter suppresses the sugar response.

*A simulation built on the real wiring of a fruit fly's brain.*
